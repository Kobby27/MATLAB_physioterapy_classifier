# MATLAB_physioterapy_classifier
Strokes often lead to indefinite neurological complications and sometimes death. Due to all body functions tracing back to the brain, neurological damage can lead to loss of muscle function, which is unfortunately the fate that befalls stroke patients (1). To combat this predicament, physical rehab is required to essentially ‘tell the brain’ to reactivate the neurons in certain muscles. Progress here is directly proportional to patient compliance. Hence, a DSS (Decision Support System) using wearable sensors was designed to track what exercise a patient is doing, how many times it is being done and whether it is being done correctly. Motion data was captured using Xsens sensors. Features from the motion such as acceleration, Root Mean Square (RMS), jerkiness etc. The movements were then classified using a machine learning model in MATLAB. The system successfully tracked movements, classified them correctly, distinguished between quality and lousy movements, and displayed the results in the DSS. Indeed, this system aids physiotherapists in effectively monitoring their patients’ progress remotely, and in turn helps the patients on their road to recovery.

The data was collected by my group members and I. We performed four different exercises: cup to mouth, lateral raise, horizontal arm stretch and 90 degrees wrist rotation.
We performed 4 sets of 10 reps for each exercise, resulting in 64 text files containing information such as acceleration, jerkiness, angular velocity etc.
